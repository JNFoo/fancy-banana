How to play

Tap, click, or press Space to flap. Fly the banana through the gaps. Every gate you pass scores 1.

Touch an obstacle or the ground and the run ends. You can pop off the top of the screen for a moment, but you get pulled back down. Every 10 gates is a new stage, and each stage you clear sets 5 banana friends free from Prince Chimp!

Grab the golden coins in the gaps. Fly through the middle of a gap for a PERFECT; chain them for streak bonuses. Skim close past an obstacle for a Close call bonus. Coins are banana credits for the Upgrades shop.

After stage 4 comes a water bonus game, Chimp's Navy, seen from above: drag your finger (or move the mouse, or use the arrow keys) to steer Fancy. Tap, click or press Space to LEAP over chimp submarines and roped barrels of chimps; you cannot steer while you are in the air, so time it. Seagulls cannot be leapt (unless you buy Super Leap): steer around them, and avoid the whirlpools, which slow you down. You have 3 hearts. Pop bubbles to free extra friends, and reach Prince Chimp's beach. The pause button (or the P key) holds the game still. If the Navy gets you, try that part again. In the Upgrades shop you can buy Super Leap and, for practice, a Practice button on the title screen. The very last stage is Prince Chimp's jungle kingdom. Beat it and you can keep your upgrades and credits or start fresh. It never gets harder.
