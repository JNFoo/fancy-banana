How to play

Tap, click, or press Space to flap. Fly the banana through the gaps. Every gate you pass scores 1.

Touch an obstacle or the ground and the run ends. You can pop off the top of the screen for a moment, but you get pulled back down. Every 10 gates is a new stage, and each stage you clear sets 5 banana friends free from Prince Chimp!
