# What's new

- **2026.10.10.1344 (alpha):** A softer, toy-like look inspired by the Apple Press style. Buttons are now **chunky and glossy** with a thick lip that squishes when you press them. The title leans slightly and sits on a soft shadow. Fancy's **blush changes with his mood** (rosy when happy, deep when surprised, pale when gritting his teeth), his eyes have a slightly bolder outline, and a cool white **rim light** and a sharper sparkle make every 3D toy pop off the background.
- **2026.10.10.1232 (alpha):** New upgrade: **Lime defense** (150 credits, then 300). In boss fights, when a coconut is about to hit you, a little rocket-propelled lime shoots out, chases the coconut and pops it (2 limes per fight for each level). It only fires in the last half second, so you still get to dodge first. Shown on the pause screen and as a small L badge while you fly.
- **2026.10.10.1213 (alpha):** Boss fights feel more like a party. **Close calls** in a fight now get a cheer and sparkles. Each time you survive a round, a big **encouraging word** pops up (Flawless! Great dodging! Banana-tastic!) with confetti, and the boss bar is now **three segments** with a star that lights as you clear each round. The **crown is now a real 3D gold crown** with pearls and jewels, on Fancy, on the crown pieces and on Prince Chimp. Mecha-Chimp's **rocket flames** are bigger and hotter, bosses kick up dust as they arrive and leave a trail of stars when beaten, and Fancy does a victory roll.
- **2026.10.10.1154 (alpha):** Boss fights get a graphics pass: all four bosses are redrawn with softer 3D lighting and more detail, the wagon hangs from the same glossy balloons as the Balloon Party (they sway and float away when you win), lasers glow, rocket fists trail flame and smoke, shockwaves look like real waves, and bosses cast a shadow on the floor. Balloons everywhere got a richer shine. **Fancy now makes an "oh!" face** (wide eyes, round mouth, a sweat drop) on a long drop, like a rollercoaster; he keeps the gritted teeth for bounces and hits. Secrets such as the sky vault can no longer open during a boss fight, and a fight never begins with the launch spiral.
- **2026.10.10.1122 (alpha):** Fights now make sense in the game world: Prince Chimp's wafers shoot out of his cannon, Mecha-Chimp hovers on rocket boots and launches its fists and lasers from its own arm and chest, the Gorilla holds a coconut and throws it, and shockwaves come from a stomp. **New: your upgrades are on show.** Pause the game to see everything switched on (upgrades with levels, free bounces left, Second wind ready or used, game speed, victory bonus, your active Challenge and fight), and a tiny row of letter badges under the stage text shows them while you fly. The pennant line is shorter and the flags sit closer together.
- **2026.10.10.1040 (alpha):** Fixed: "Keep and go to title" on the first victory screen now ends on the title screen (it used to land on the launch pad after Prince Chimp's gift). The pennant trail is smaller and more lifelike: a thin curved string with small flags that stream back and flutter more toward the loose end.
- **2026.10.10.0934 (alpha):** Fight polish. Bosses land with a thump when they arrive, and a beaten boss spins away crying. The victory card and the Challenges panel now show your three crown pieces (and the whole crown after the Crown Duel).
- **2026.10.10.0545 (alpha):** **Prince Chimp fights!** Beat the game twice, then turn on **Prince Chimp fight** in Challenges. In your next run the last two gates of the final stage give way to a boss: the **Wafer Cannon** wagon, then **Mecha-Chimp** (lasers and rocket fists), then the **Gorilla Guard** (coconuts and shockwaves), then the **Crown Duel** (everything at once). One fight per playthrough, in that order. Each fight has three 12-second rounds; if you get hit, only that round repeats. Warnings show where each attack will land. Beat the first three for crown pieces; win the Duel to get Prince Chimp's true ending and the **Banana Crown** for Fancy (Costume). The Free bounce upgrade absorbs one hit.
- **2026.10.10.0049 (alpha):** **Challenges!** Once you have beaten Prince Chimp, a Challenges button appears on the title screen. Pick one for your next run: **Night flashlight** (everything is dark except a circle of light round Fancy), **Tiny gaps** (every opening 20% smaller), **Wafer storm** (vanilla wafers float between the gates) or **Mirror** (the world is upside down). Beat the whole game in one to earn its **badge** and a new **scarf color and mask color** for Costume. Normal runs and Practice never change, and the hidden rooms stay shut during a challenge. Help and the title ticker hint at it.
- **2026.10.10.0032 (alpha):** Fixes and comfort. The water level's finish card now names the right next stage (it was naming the wrong one). **Prince Chimp cries** comically on the victory screen ("Boo hoo!") and when he surrenders his balloons. **Controller:** the d-pad or sticks steer in the water level, and now also move around every menu (title buttons, Settings, Help, Save, Costume, Upgrades, Practice, victory choices): A picks, B goes back, Start closes; on a pause screen Y opens Settings. **Help** now has a picture for every tip, a Secrets section with rumors, and the title ticker drops hints about the secrets.
- **2026.10.10.0011 (alpha):** Secrets! Once you have beaten Prince Chimp, the game starts hiding things (there is a little hint under Help). **Rooms full of bananas:** fly along the very top edge in the quiet stretch after a stage, stay low before the Silverware Drawer, or pass a glowing gold gate after a long perfect streak. **Six lost banana friends** hide between gates from your third playthrough; find all six for the **Banana Bunch** costume. **Five quick taps on Fancy** on the title screen open the **Banana Museum**. **Chimp's Navy:** a clean leap over a thrown wafer now pays a bonus banana (and a bigger one for each clean clear in a run), plus a treasure raft, a secret rock arch, and a giant submarine for a flawless run. Two new upgrades: **Rope cutter** and **Wafer shield**.
- **2026.10.09.2349 (alpha):** Lots of comfort, plus new upgrades. **Continue** brings you back to the stage you had reached if the tab was closed mid-run. **Game speed** now has 100, 90, 80, 70 and 60% (each keeps its own best score). **Larger text**, a **Settings** button on the pause screen, **game controller** support, a **Copy my result** button on the victory screen, and **Keep and go to title** so you can rest after a win. A little flag on the progress dots shows where your best run ended. From your **5th win**, the last two stages are 15 gates long. New upgrades: **Banana magnet**, **Sweet spot** (wider PERFECT zone), **Daredevil** (wider close calls), **Golden touch** and **Second wind** (once per run, a crash restarts the stage instead of ending the run). In the Balloon Party, quick **chains** of pops pay extra. A **rainbow trail** unlocks with a perfect streak of 10, and the title screen shows a trophy at +50% and +100% speed.
- **2026.10.09.2331 (alpha):** Smoother start to every stage, especially on Android phones. The picture-building that used to land in the first seconds of a new stage is now spread out in tiny pieces and waits for a quiet frame, so there should be far fewer hiccups when a stage begins.
- **2026.10.09.2247 (alpha):** Secrets and a bigger finish. **Beat Prince Chimp the first time** and he surrenders with a white flag and hands over his whole balloon collection: that unlocks the **Balloon Party**, a calm bonus stage between the Ice Cream Parlor and the Silverware Drawer (no obstacles; pop glossy 3D balloons for credits, gold ones are worth 5; the floor is the only thing that can hurt you), and **pennant flags** that fly behind Fancy (Costume panel to turn them on or off). **From your third playthrough**, the submarines and barrel chimps throw vanilla wafers at Fancy in the water level: leap over them or steer around (each wafer has a shadow). **Every time you win and keep your upgrades, the whole game is 10% faster** (up to double); starting fresh resets that. The victory card is now a full celebration with fireworks, a medal, a hopping Fancy and stats that fly in: time, pauses, taps, credits, hits taken, friends freed, balloons popped, records, and your secrets. Also fixed: after a stage clear the first gate could arrive too soon after GO, so it now comes about a second later and opens right where Fancy waits. New tab icon (Fancy's face). The first screen appears about twice as fast, and the stage pictures build with fewer graphics-card stalls.
- **2026.10.09.2158 (alpha):** Fixed the thin horizontal lines on the candy cane pillars (they showed on screens scaled to 125% or 150%). Settings is now compact and fits on one screen without scrolling at any size. The "GO!" word is much bigger.
- **2026.10.09.2143 (alpha):** More speed and safety. The game file is about 9% smaller. A **Battery saver** option in Settings draws about 30 pictures a second and keeps the game itself exactly the same. If a screen struggles, the game quietly lightens itself (fewer sparks and drifting decorations, then a slightly softer picture) and recovers when things calm down. Resizing the window or a phone's address bar sliding no longer causes a flurry of rebuilds, and if a device runs out of picture memory the game draws a little softer instead of going black.
- **2026.10.09.2127 (alpha):** Firefox tuning. The news ticker on the title screen is cheaper to draw, white fades are lighter, and a paused game now rests instead of redrawing itself (less heat and battery). Firefox frames are a little faster; Chromium is unchanged.
- **2026.10.09.2105 (alpha):** Ten comfort upgrades. **Settings** (gear button): Calm mode (no flashes, shake or zoom; it switches on by itself if your device asks for less motion), Relaxed speed (80% speed with its own best score), left-handed layout, large buttons, the news ticker, and full screen. After a pause there is a 3-2-1 before play carries on. Taps show a little ring, and a tap just before Fancy can respond is remembered. A row of ten dots shows how many gates are left in the stage and the best score to beat.
